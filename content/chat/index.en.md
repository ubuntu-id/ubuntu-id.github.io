# Choose where to chat
You can chat with other Ubuntu users in Indonesia through the channels below. Pick whichever is most comfortable for you.

- **Telegram**: [Ubuntu Indonesia group](https://t.me/ubuntu_indo) | [Announcement channel](https://t.me/ubuntu_id)
- **Facebook**: [Ubuntu Indonesia group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/2327054593)
- **Mailing list**: [id-ubuntu on Google Groups](https://groups.google.com/group/id-ubuntu)
- **Forum**: [Ubuntu Discourse](https://discourse.ubuntu.com/c/community/circles/ubuntu-id/133)

For help in English, you can also ask on [Ask Ubuntu](https://askubuntu.com/).
