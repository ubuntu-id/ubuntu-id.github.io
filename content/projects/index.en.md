# Group Studies/Projects
![study](study.png)
We regularly hold group studies on topics about Ubuntu or related open source technologies.
Costs, space, equipment, and so on needed for a study are supported by the community or sponsored separately.

Study topics are proposed by members or planned and chosen by the management team, and members are recruited through the forum or social media channels.

- [Wiki](https://wiki.ubuntu.com)
- [GitHub Organization](https://github.com/ubuntu-id)

# Projects
![project](project.png)
We take part in open source projects on Ubuntu or related open source technologies, or develop new projects.
Likewise, the resources needed to take part in or develop a project are supported by the community or sponsored separately.

- [Wiki](https://wiki.ubuntu.com)
- [GitHub Organization](https://github.com/ubuntu-id)
